The story behind the lens

I'm a passionate photographer who believes every moment tells a unique story. Born and raised in Mumbai, I developed an eye for capturing the extraordinary in the ordinary from an early age.

My journey into photography began in 2021. What started as a hobby quickly evolved into a lifelong passion and career dedicated to preserving memories through my lens.

Whether it's the intimate emotions of a wedding day, the joy of family gatherings, the energy of corporate events, or the serene beauty of landscapes, I use my camera to freeze these precious moments in time. Each occasion presents its own story, and I'm here to capture yours with authenticity and artistry.

From weddings and engagements to portraits and corporate events, I bring the same dedication to every shoot. My approach focuses on natural, candid moments while ensuring every important detail is beautifully preserved for you to cherish forever.
